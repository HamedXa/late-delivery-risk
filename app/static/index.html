Late-delivery risk at carrier handoff

Enter an order at the moment the seller hands the parcel to the carrier. The model returns a risk score, where that score ranks against recent orders, and what is driving it. The JSON API is documented at /docs.

Fill in the order and press the button, or pick one of the three examples.

The score ranks orders. It is not a calibrated probability: its level drifts when the network's late rate shifts, so alerts go to the riskiest 10% of recent orders. Trained on the public Olist dataset (Brazil, 2016 to 2018); a demonstration, not a production service.
